Supported Patterns

8 detection categories

The scanner classifies webpage elements according to the following dark-pattern and interface categories.

01 Urgency Fake countdown timers and time-pressure warnings.
02 Scarcity Artificial stock limits and high-demand claims.
03 Confirmshaming Manipulative option copy intended to induce guilt.
04 Forced Action Mandatory signups, notifications, or forced prerequisites.
05 Preselection Optional terms, settings, or tracking selected by default.
06 Misdirection Visual tricks, disguised controls, and confusing wording.
07 Privacy Manipulation Deceptive cookie controls, forced tracking, and difficult opt-outs.
08 Normal UI Standard interface elements without identifiable deception.
